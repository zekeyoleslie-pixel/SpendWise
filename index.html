Data & Help

Protect your records and understand how SpendWise stores your information.

Backup and restore

Download a JSON backup containing your expenses, budgets, custom categories, and selected period. Keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a backup replaces the current data in this browser.

Backups are stored wherever you choose to save the downloaded file.

Restart SpendWise

Clear all saved expenses, budgets, and custom categories in this browser and return the app to its starting state. Default categories will remain available. This action cannot be undone unless you have a backup.

Export a backup first if you may need your existing records later.

Your data stays in this browser

SpendWise currently saves your records in this browser. It does not sync automatically across devices or provide cloud backup.

Tip: export a backup before clearing browser data, changing devices, or making major updates.
